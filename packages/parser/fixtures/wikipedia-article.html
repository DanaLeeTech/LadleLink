Pancake

A pancake is a flat cake, often thin and round, prepared from a starch-based batter.

History

Pancakes are ancient food. The earliest attested pancakes date back to antiquity.

Etymology

The word "pancake" derives from the Middle English "panne" and "cake".

Varieties

  • Crêpe
  • Pikelet
  • Dosa
  • Blin

Preparation

Batter is poured onto a hot griddle and cooked on both sides. Some recipes add 1 cup of buttermilk, but proportions vary by region and household.
