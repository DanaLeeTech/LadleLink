Beef Ragu

4 servings · 15 minutes prep · 2 hours 30 minutes cook

Ingredients

  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 500 g beef chuck, cut into 3cm pieces
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 x 400g cans crushed tomatoes
  • 250 ml beef stock

Instructions

  1. Heat the olive oil in a heavy pot over high heat.
  2. Brown the beef in batches, then set aside.
  3. Cook the onion and garlic, add the tomatoes and stock, then return the beef.
  4. Cover and simmer for 2 hours 30 minutes until the beef falls apart.
