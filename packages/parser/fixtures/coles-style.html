Chocolate Chip Cookies

Serves12

Prep 15m · Cook 12m

Ingredients

For the dough

  • 2 cups plain flour
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • 125 g butter, softened

For the icing

  • 1 cup icing sugar
  • 2 tbsp milk

Method

  1. Step 1. Preheat the oven to 180°C and line two trays.
  2. 2. Mix the dough, roll into balls and bake for 12 minutes.
